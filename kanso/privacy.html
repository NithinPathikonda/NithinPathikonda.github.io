Privacy Policy for Kanso: Habit Tracker

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Kanso ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from the ground up with a privacy-first, local-first philosophy. We believe your daily habits, personal routines, and streak data belong exclusively to you.

1. Zero Data Collection

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers.
  • All routines, completed dates, streaks, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device's internal storage using sandboxed AsyncStorage.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
  • Operating System Cloud Backups: On Android devices where you have Google Backup enabled, Android Auto Backup may silently back up the app's local database to your personal, private Google Drive account. We (the developers) never have access to this backup; it is encrypted and managed entirely between your device and Google under your personal Google account settings.

2. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Analytics

  • The app contains no advertising SDKs (no Google AdMob, Unity, etc.).
  • The app contains no third-party telemetry or analytics trackers (no Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Mixpanel, or Sentry).
  • Kanso makes zero network calls during normal operation and works 100% offline.

3. Device Permissions

  • Kanso requests zero dangerous device permissions.
  • The app does not access your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Photo Library, SMS, or Location.

4. Data Portability & Deletion

  • Data Portability: You can export a full copy of your habit data in standard JSON format at any time directly from the Preferences & Hub menu.
  • Data Deletion: You can permanently delete all data stored by the app at any time by selecting "Reset All Local Data" in Preferences, or by clearing the app's cache or uninstalling it from your device.

5. In-App Purchases (Supporter Club)

  • If you choose to support independent development by tipping via the Supporter Club, payments are processed directly and securely by Google Play (for Android) or Apple App Store (for iOS). We never see, receive, or store your credit card or financial details.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

  • Because Kanso collects no personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/NithinPathikonda/kanso
